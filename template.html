The Originals — a no-movement universe. Every player stays forever with the organization that first acquired him: the club that drafted him (2000–2025), signed him as an international or undrafted amateur, or gave him his first MLB contract. Prospects traded before debuting credit their first org. The table shows what each franchise's originals are producing in 2026 and the record that talent would support — replacement level is ~48 wins per 162, pro-rated from the games played at build time. Raw WAR, so slumps and injuries count. Rosters are uneven by design; this is a scoreboard of who talent actually belongs to.

The best team money can buy. Set a payroll and the optimizer assembles the highest-WAR legal 26-man roster from every 2026 player: one at each infield spot and catcher, three outfielders, a DH, four bench bats, and thirteen pitchers, maximizing this season's WAR under your budget. Salaries are Baseball-Reference's listed 2026 figures; unlisted pre-arb players are priced at the $780K minimum, and deferred or backloaded deals use the listed number, so some superstars are bargains. Projected record uses the same replacement baseline as the Originals tab. Heuristic optimizer — near-optimal, not certified. Fair warning: pre-arbitration salaries are so low that even a ~$25M budget buys a 162-win-pace roster — that gap between pay and production is the entire economics of team building, and it's why the record chip caps at 162–0. Prospect comps. Every qualified minor leaguer this season, matched against twenty seasons of same-level history by statistical shape — K%, BB%, power, production, speed, all normalized within league-season — with age versus level weighted heaviest. Each comp is scored on WAR through his first six MLB seasons and mapped to the 2–8 role scale (R8 elite · R7 frequent All-Star · R6 occasional All-Star · R5 solid everyday or mid-rotation · R4 role player · R3 up-and-down · R2 organizational). A prospect's projection comes from his 25 nearest matured comps (seasons through __MAT__, so their six years are in): the grade is the highest role at least 26% of the cohort reached, tagged Poor (0–25) · Fair (26–49) · Good (50–74) · Excellent (75–100) by that probability. This is a triage tool built from public stat lines: it can't see swings, defense, makeup, or medicals — it points eyes and quantifies priors.
